 WELCOME TO FLOORPLAN-LAB

A small space.
Many possibilities.

  1. Define your room. Enter exact width and depth in millimetres.
  2. Divide the space. Create two areas and select either one on the drawing.
  3. Choose a pattern. Open the Layout Library for aligned, offset, herringbone, basket weave or hexagonal layouts. Adjust size, joints, rotation and origin.
  4. Move the pattern. Choose Move layout and drag inside an area. Escape cancels a drag; Undo restores the previous origin.
  5. Find a better fit. Choose Optimize layout, run the search and compare before applying.
  6. Keep your work. Save JSON to reopen later; export SVG for a vector drawing.

Version 0.3 supports rectangular rooms, aligned/offset layouts, herringbone, basket weave and regular hexagons. CAD import, freeform rooms and offcut reuse are not yet implemented. Projects stay in this browser unless you export them.
