WELCOME TO FLOORPLAN-LAB

A small space.
Many possibilities.

  1. Define your room. Enter exact width and depth in millimetres.
  2. Divide the space. Create two areas and select either one on the drawing.
  3. Explore a layout. Adjust tile size, joints, rotation and origin.
  4. Keep your work. Save JSON to reopen later; export SVG for a vector drawing.

This first release supports rectangular rooms and rectangular pieces. CAD import, freeform rooms, hexagons and herringbone are planned, not yet implemented. Projects stay in this browser unless you export them.
